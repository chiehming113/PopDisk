# PopDisk Privacy

PopDisk does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, or ads.

## What PopDisk connects to

- **GitHub, to check for updates.** Once a day (and when you press "Check for Updates…"), PopDisk downloads a small file listing the latest version from `raw.githubusercontent.com`. When you choose to install an update, it downloads it from `github.com`. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; PopDisk sends nothing else. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings → General.
- **Your own network drives.** PopDisk connects to the NAS or servers you add, and briefly opens a connection to measure their latency. This traffic stays between your Mac and your server.

## What stays on your Mac

- Network drive passwords are stored only in your Mac's keychain.
- Nicknames, icons, ordering, favorites, and other settings are stored in PopDisk's preferences on your Mac.

## Reporting a problem

"Copy System Info" (Settings → About) copies only the PopDisk and macOS versions, your Mac model and chip, and the interface language. Nothing is sent until you paste it into a report yourself.
